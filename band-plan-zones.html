WW Digi Band Plan

When each band opens and closes to every CQ zone, over the night of 29–30 August 2026. Measured as offsets from the terminator that bounds each path, then resolved against real sunrise and sunset for this date — which is why it can be drawn for a night that falls after the archive ends.

Sun at FN32: day civil nautical astronomical night ridge color is the field's position — hue is its longitude band, lightness its latitude · ridge height is that half-hour against that path's own busiest half-hour (), drawn wherever it reaches 2% of that peak — so a real closure goes flat and a merely quiet hour dips the shaded number is that row's share of its own continent, so each block sums to 100% and is shaded against its own range; the percentage on each continent heading is that continent's share of the whole band, which is where two blocks can be compared

Edges are the 5th and 95th percentiles of each path's spot distribution — where real activity starts and stops, not the last straggler.

Recency: a spot counts as exp(−age ⁄ 27.2753 days), the Carrington rotation, matching the weighting the SNR heat maps use — one from today counts 1.0, one from a rotation ago 0.37. Spot totals here are therefore recent-equivalent; each row's tooltip carries the raw count too. Against the unweighted sweep the median opening moves 6–18 min, but 17% of rows move over an hour and 5% over three, almost all of them sparse rows whose effective sample the weighting cuts by 3.2×.

Seasonal caveat: the offsets come from 20 May – 11 Aug 2026, which brackets the June solstice, so the season does not drift one way across the window — day length spans just 74 minutes, folded about the solstice. The target night is the harder part: at 69 days past the solstice it sits outside both limbs, 1.7 h shorter than the window's mean day. That is why the edges are measured against the terminator rather than in UTC — sunset moves from 00:32Z to 23:30Z across that gap, and only the offsets carry over. A December night would not. Re-run the sweep quarterly.
