How this was built

Three datasets, none sufficient alone, merged on the contest clock:

  • All 10,328 public WW Digi logs, 2019–2025 — both sides of every QSO, so log submissions prove who entered this contest from where, and the stations they worked reveal the participants who never submitted. Hourly patterns use 2023–25 only: the solar-minimum years affected the high bands too differently (hourly-shape correlation 0.3–0.5 on 40/15/10m) to pool.
  • An 84-day archive of spots to/from FN32 ± 1 square (230M rows, rolling window through 19 August), reduced to terminator-relative opening/closing edges per grid field and band, weighted by solar rotation (a spot counts exp(−age/27.28 d)), then projected onto the actual contest night — which is how a summer archive can describe a night after it ends.
  • An August-only re-sweep at the contest's forecast solar flux (SWPC: F10.7 ≈ 110 for the weekend vs the archive's 132 mean; the extended August window now averages 112, right at the forecast): 15m holds its shape at ~55% depth; 10m runs at roughly a quarter of archive rates but stays broad — South America and the Caribbean carry the volume, W-North America holds up best against the archive, and southern Europe still produces at reduced rates; only northern Europe all but vanishes. One point in the weekend's favor: binned by Carrington rotation, the flux drop is a step — CR 2310–2313 (15 Apr – 2 Aug) held a stable 124–134 median before CR 2314 (3–29 Aug) fell to ~100 — yet the contest days land on the rotation's most persistent longitude (Carrington longitude ≈ 0°, crossing the central meridian near the contest's midpoint), whose recurrence at the same phase produced SFI of 125–139 for three straight rotations. SWPC's 110 sits between that recurrence and the new baseline; expect the friendlier side of the 15m fall off.

Standing decisions baked into every page: FP and GO carry regular beacon traffic but only 3 contest participants each in seven years — planned around, not for; GN (29 participants) and HP (23) are the bankable thin multipliers; grid fields are assigned to CQ zones from the WAZ map with boundary fields settled by where their spots actually originate.
