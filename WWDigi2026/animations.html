Reading the map

Bearing reads straight off the map (north up); distance is radial, with the dashed orange rings marking the WW Digi point steps (one point per started 3000 km). A rolling 30-minute window slides one minute per frame, 1440 frames looping the day from 1200Z to 1200Z — the contest clock. Night is shaded for the contest date, auroral ovals are the nominal Kp≈2 extent, and dot size and opacity are linear in log spot count. Append #f=N to a page URL to open it paused N minutes after 1200Z.
